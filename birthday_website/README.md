# 💖 Romantic Birthday Website for Boyfriend

A personalized, interactive, aesthetic Birthday Wishing Website crafted with love.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **🎁 Mysterious Surprise Opening (First Page)**:
   - 3D gift box unwrap animation with stardust and confetti fanfare.
   - Smooth transition into the main celebration.

2. **🎵 Dedicated Song Experience (Vinyl Music Station)**:
   - Spinning vinyl record player with album artwork.
   - Customized quote, lyrics highlight, interactive play/pause, time bar, and floating audio player.

3. **🎂 Interactive Birthday Cake & Candles**:
   - Realistic multi-tiered cake with animated flickering candle flames.
   - Click to blow out the candles, producing realistic smoke, celebratory chime sound, and a vibrant confetti explosion!

4. **📸 Polaroid Scrapbook / Memories Gallery**:
   - Aesthetic tilted polaroid photos with tape effects.
   - Click to open a flip modal and read the secret handwritten note on the back of each memory.

5. **💌 Wax-Sealed Romantic Love Letter**:
   - 3D folding envelope with a ruby-and-gold wax heart seal.
   - Break the seal to unfold a parchment love letter with glowing typography.

6. **🫙 "Reasons Why I Love You" Jar**:
   - Interactive jar to draw sweet, funny, and romantic reasons one by one.

7. **🎟️ Redeemable Birthday Coupons**:
   - Free massage, late night drives, movie choice, favorite meal, and unlimited hugs vouchers that he can click to stamp as "REDEEMED".

8. **🌌 Aesthetic Floating Stars & Sparkle Trail**:
   - Dreamy starry background with mouse/touch sparkle trails.

## ✏️ How to Customize (Easy!)

Open **`config.js`** in any text editor to customize:
- **Boyfriend's Name & Nickname** (`boyfriendName`, `nickname`)
- **Dedicated Song** (`title`, `artist`, `audioSrc`, `albumCover`, `dedicationNote`)
- **Your Own Photos & Memories** (`memories` array with photo links, dates, captions, and secret back notes)
- **Love Letter** (`letter.paragraphs`, `salutation`, `signature`)
- **Reasons Why You Love Him** (`reasons` list)
- **Love Coupons** (`coupons` list)
